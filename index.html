About Me

I am a senior in Aerospace Engineering with minors in Music and Global Engineering. I am acting as the lead airframe and aerodynamics engineer for my Senior Projects focused on autonomous UAS systems. Our project aims to create a quadrotor with the ability to survey a designated area and determine the location of a payload. The payload location will be passed to a rover for retrieval, built by our classmates in a separate team. I will be completing both of my minors in Fall 2026. Throughout my music minor, I have been elated to return to playing my flute and learning more about playing the tenor saxophone. I have also been able to learn about music technology and the relation of music to global health. In my Global Engineering minor, I was lucky to be able to continue my education in the Spanish language through multiple conversation based courses as well as taking courses on intercultural communication in the workplace. I was also honored to be able to go abroad to Amsterdam in May 2026 through the Leeds School of Business to learn about New Product Development. I also founded TenTrainers, a 3D printed training knife company originating from my dad needing new trainers for his defense training. I personally design the trainers and manufacture them to order.
